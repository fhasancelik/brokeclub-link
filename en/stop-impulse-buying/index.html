How to stop impulse buying

The urge to buy fades. Let it.

Most impulse buys happen in a short window: you see it, you want it, you tap buy. Stretch that window and the urge usually fades first. That is the idea behind Craving SOS.

Hit SOS before checkout

Open the Coach tab and tap I want to buy something. Pick a category, name the item and type the price. Talk me out of it shows what that habit costs over a week and a year, then suggests three things to do instead.

Outlast it

Start the five-minute urge timer, or play Penny's Pocket, a catch-the-coins game, until the feeling passes. Held off? Tap I resisted and the money joins your kept total. Caved? The app notes it and moves on. No shame.

Park it for 48 hours

Can't decide? Park it for 48 hours, 3 days or 7 days. When time is up, a notification asks if you still want it. Let it go and the price counts as kept. Buy it and that's fine too: you waited and decided. Tell the club asks friends to stop you.

How it works

  1. Open Coach and tap I want to buy something.
  2. Enter the category, the item and the price.
  3. Read the reality check, then start the timer.
  4. Resist, cave, or Park it and decide later.

Questions

Why do I keep impulse buying?

Sales, one-tap checkout and boredom push you to buy fast. The feeling is strong but short, so a pause between wanting and paying breaks the loop.

What is the 48-hour rule?

Wait 48 hours before buying anything unplanned. Still want it after two days? Buy it with a clear head. Park it does the waiting and reminds you.

Can Broke Club help with a shopping addiction?

It helps you build pauses and see what you didn't spend, but it isn't treatment. If shopping feels out of control or causes debt or distress, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional.
