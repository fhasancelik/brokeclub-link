No-spend challenges, played with friends

Saving money, loudly.

Broke Club turns not buying stuff into a game you play with your friends. Pick a challenge, check in once a day and watch the money you didn't spend pile up.

One tap a day keeps the streak alive

Every challenge has a daily amount: the coffee, delivery or late-night cart you would normally pay for. Each evening you answer Held the line or I slipped. Held days grow your streak and the total you kept. Slipped days are marked on the calendar, no lecture attached.

Built for the moment you want to buy

Craving SOS is one tap away. Tell it what you want and what it costs, and the coach shows what that habit adds up to over a year, suggests three things to do instead and starts a five-minute urge timer. Still unsure? Park it for 48 hours. More in how to stop impulse buying.

Friends, a budget and Penny

Share a BRK code and friends join the same challenge, with a live leaderboard and optional stakes. The Budget tab shows income, bills, spending and what is left until payday. Pro members can also chat with Penny, the AI coach.

How it works

  1. Pick a challenge: no takeout coffee, a No Buy Month, no Amazon or your own rules.
  2. Set the daily amount and the length: 7, 14 or 30 days.
  3. Check in every day with Held the line or I slipped.
  4. Invite friends with your BRK code and race on the leaderboard.

Questions

How much does Broke Club cost?

Broke Club is a Pro subscription, monthly or yearly. The price for your country is shown in the app before you buy, and you can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Does Broke Club connect to my bank?

No. Broke Club never touches your money or asks for bank logins. You tell it what you skipped and what it would have cost, and it keeps the score.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on the App Store and Google Play. The app comes in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Arabic.
