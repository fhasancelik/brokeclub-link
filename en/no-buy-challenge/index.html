No buy challenge tracker

Your No Buy Month, tracked one day at a time.

A no buy challenge means buying nothing that isn't essential for a set time, usually a month. Rent, groceries and bills still get paid. The new hoodie and the 2 a.m. cart do not.

Set the rules before day one

Broke Club has a ready-made No Buy Month template: 30 days, one daily amount, one check-in a day. Want your own rules? Build a custom challenge with up to eight exceptions, such as groceries or medicine, so what is allowed is written down.

Narrow it down if a month feels big

Not every no buy has to be total. Try No impulse shopping, No Amazon, No TikTok Shop, or Shop your closet, where you wear what you already own. Runs of 7 or 14 days make a good warm-up, and Craving SOS helps when an urge gets loud.

See what the month was worth

Every held day adds its daily amount to your kept total. Stats shows that total, a seven-day chart and progress towards a goal like a trip. A weekly Bank it card reminds you to move the money into savings.

How it works

  1. Pick the No Buy Month template or start a custom challenge.
  2. List the essentials you will still buy.
  3. Check in every evening with Held the line or I slipped.
  4. Move what you kept into savings when Bank it asks each week.

Questions

What can I buy during a no buy challenge?

Essentials: housing, bills, groceries, medicine and getting to work. Everything else waits. A custom challenge lets you list your own exceptions.

How long should a no buy challenge last?

A month is the classic length; the No Buy Month template runs 30 days. If that feels like a lot, start with 7 or 14.

Can I do a No Buy Month with friends?

Yes. Share the challenge code and friends join with a live leaderboard. Add stakes if you like: loser buys Friday coffee.
