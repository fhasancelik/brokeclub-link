No-Spend January

No-Spend January: 31 days, one check-in each.

After December, plenty of people decide to stop spending for a while. No-Spend January is the big one in Broke Club: a seasonal event from January 1 to 31 where the whole club buys nothing it doesn't need.

How to join

In December a No-Spend January card appears on the Club tab with a countdown. From January 1 you can tap Join the event, and the app builds a challenge for the rest of the month with its own calendar, streak and kept total. Join on the 10th and it simply runs from the 10th.

Making 31 days doable

A month is long, so set yourself up. Write down your essentials first: rent, groceries, medicine. Use Craving SOS when an urge hits and Park it for anything you are unsure about. Set a goal in Stats, like a trip or an emergency fund, so the streak has a reason.

Prefer September?

There's a second round every year. No-Spend September runs September 1 to 30, for when summer has spent your money. Both events work the same way, and you can start your own 30-day challenge, or a No Buy Month, in any month.

How it works

  1. Install Broke Club before New Year.
  2. On January 1, tap Join the event on the Club tab.
  3. Check in each day with Held the line or I slipped.
  4. Open Stats at the end of the month to see what January kept.

Questions

What is No-Spend January?

A challenge to spend only on essentials for the whole of January. In Broke Club it is a seasonal event that runs January 1 to 31.

Can I start No-Spend January late?

Yes. While the event is live you can join on any day, and your challenge runs from that day to January 31.

What counts as essential?

Housing, bills, groceries, medicine and getting to work are the usual list. Coffee runs, delivery and new clothes are what most people cut.

How much does Broke Club cost?

You can try Broke Club free for 7 days. After that it continues as a Pro subscription, and the price for your country is shown in the app before you start. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
